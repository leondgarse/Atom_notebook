## Week 1 Summary
- **Linear Algebra Fundamentals 線性代數基礎**
  - **Vector Dot Product:** Measures geometric similarity. A dot product of zero indicates orthogonal (perpendicular) vectors, meaning zero correlation between those data points.
  - **Linear Independence:** Critical for selecting regression features. Linearly dependent columns represent redundant information, which confuses the model and prevents it from uniquely assigning feature weights.
  - **Matrix Systems:** Understanding rank and inverses is essential for solving linear systems (Ax = b). Full rank ensures a unique solution, mathematically guaranteeing that our algorithms can find an optimal weight combination.
  - **向量內積：** 衡量幾何相似度。內積為零表示向量正交（垂直），意味著這些數據點之間完全沒有相關性。
  - **線性獨立：** 對於選擇迴歸特徵至關重要。線性相關的行（columns）代表冗餘資訊，這會混淆模型並阻止它唯一地分配特徵權重。
  - **矩陣系統：** 了解秩（rank）和反矩陣對於求解線性系統 (Ax = b) 至關重要。滿秩確保了唯一解，在數學上保證了我們的演算法能夠找到最佳的權重組合。
- **Calculus and Optimization 微積分與最佳化**
  - **Taylor Series Approximation:** Deep learning loss functions are too complex to minimize directly. We use Taylor polynomials to locally approximate these landscapes as simple quadratic bowls (stopping at degree 2) to compute descent steps.
  - **The Chain Rule:** The mathematical engine behind backpropagation. It allows us to compute the gradient of composite functions across multiple network layers by passing derivatives backward from the output error to the input weights.
  - **Gradients:** The vector of partial derivatives (∇). In multivariable optimization, moving in the negative direction of the gradient points us down the steepest path of the loss surface.
  - **泰勒級數逼近：** 深度學習的損失函數太過複雜，無法直接最小化。我們使用泰勒多項式在局部將這些地形近似為簡單的二次碗狀結構（停留在二次方），以計算下降步長。
  - **連鎖律：** 反向傳播背後的數學引擎。它允許我們透過將導數從輸出誤差向後傳遞到輸入權重，來計算跨越多個網路層的複合函數梯度。
  - **梯度：** 偏導數的向量 (∇)。在多變數最佳化中，沿著梯度的負方向移動會引導我們走下損失表面最陡峭的路徑。
- **Activation Functions and Network Dynamics 激勵函數與網路動態**
  - **Sigmoid Function:** Maps any real number (from negative to positive infinity) strictly into a 0 to 1 range, converting raw linear combinations into actionable classification probabilities.
  - **Derivative Behavior:** The derivative of the sigmoid is sigma * (1 - sigma), which maxes out at 0.25.
  - **Vanishing Gradient Problem:** A critical pitfall. When the model is highly confident (predictions near 0 or 1), the derivative approaches zero. This zero multiplies backward through the chain rule, killing the error signal and permanently halting weight updates.
  - **Sigmoid 函數：** 將任何實數（從負無限大到正無限大）嚴格映射到 0 到 1 的範圍內，將原始的線性組合轉換為可操作的分類機率。
  - **導數行為：** Sigmoid 的導數是 sigma * (1 - sigma)，其最大值僅為 0.25。
  - **梯度消失問題：** 一個關鍵的陷阱。當模型高度自信（預測值接近 0 或 1）時，導數趨近於零。這個零透過連鎖律向後相乘，抹殺了誤差訊號，並永久停止權重更新。
## Week 1 Learning Tips
Welcome to the foundational mathematics of AI! I know diving into linear algebra and calculus can feel abstract at first, but I want to encourage you to look past the formulas and focus on what these concepts actually *do* mechanically. Don't fall into the common pitfall of memorizing equations blindly. Instead, build your geometric intuition. When you see a dot product, visualize the angle between two customer profiles; when you see a matrix multiplication, visualize a transformation of features. Translating algebraic symbols into geometric actions will make the math feel incredibly intuitive.

歡迎來到 AI 的基礎數學領域！我知道一開始接觸線性代數和微積分可能會覺得很抽象，但我想鼓勵你們看透這些公式，將注意力集中在這些概念在機制上實際上*做*了什麼。不要陷入盲目死記硬背方程式的常見陷阱。相反地，建立你的幾何直覺。當你看到內積時，想像兩個客戶輪廓之間的角度；當你看到矩陣乘法時，想像特徵的轉換。將代數符號轉化為幾何動作，會讓數學變得非常直觀。

One of the most important concepts to master this week is the "why" behind the vanishing gradient problem. It is easy to just memorize that "sigmoid causes vanishing gradients," but I want you to understand the underlying mechanics. When your network is overly confident and pushes a net input to an extreme value, the curve of the sigmoid flattens out entirely. A flat curve means a derivative of zero. Because backpropagation relies completely on the chain rule—which is just a long sequence of multiplications—a single zero in that chain destroys the entire error signal. Understanding this connection between the calculus (the chain rule) and the network architecture (the activation function) is what separates a mere user of machine learning tools from a true AI engineer.

本週要掌握的最重要概念之一，是梯度消失問題背後的「為什麼」。我們很容易只記住「sigmoid 會導致梯度消失」，但我希望你們理解其潛在的機制。當你的網路過度自信並將淨輸入推向極值時，sigmoid 的曲線會完全變平。平坦的曲線意味著導數為零。因為反向傳播完全依賴於連鎖律——這只是一長串的乘法——這條鏈中的一個零就會破壞整個誤差訊號。理解微積分（連鎖律）和網路架構（激勵函數）之間的這種聯繫，正是區分一般機器學習工具使用者與真正 AI 工程師的關鍵。

Finally, embrace the manual math while you are learning. Yes, modern frameworks like PyTorch or TensorFlow handle backpropagation automatically with a single line of code, and you can easily ask AI to write it for you. However, you must understand the math to troubleshoot the black box. When your training loss plateaus unexpectedly, or your neural network stops learning completely, your coding skills won't save you—your understanding of gradients, matrix rank, and local minimums will. Treat these mathematical foundations not just as academic exercises, but as your primary diagnostic tools for real-world AI development.

最後，在學習的過程中，擁抱手動計算的數學吧。是的，像 PyTorch 或 TensorFlow 這樣的現代框架可以用一行程式碼自動處理反向傳播，你也可以輕鬆地讓 AI 幫你寫程式碼。然而，你必須了解數學才能對這個黑盒子進行故障排除。當你的訓練損失意外停滯，或者你的神經網路完全停止學習時，你的寫程式技巧救不了你——你對梯度、矩陣秩和局部最小值的理解才能救你。不要只將這些數學基礎視為學術練習，而是要將它們視為你在現實世界 AI 開發中的主要診斷工具。
## Week 1 Lecture
  - [13:24:25 - 13:26:36] **Vectors, Transpose, and Basic Operations**

    When dealing with data in AI and machine learning, we often represent information as vectors. Imagine we have a customer, and we have gathered 1,000 pieces of information about this specific individual. This collection of data points can be neatly organized into a vector with 1,000 components. Normally, when we say "vector," we are referring to a column vector. The components or elements within this vector, let's call it x, are denoted by x_1 through x_n. We mathematically define R^n as an n-dimensional Euclidean space, which is just standard notation to denote the collection of all possible vectors with n real-number components. For example, a vector [1, 2, 3] is a three-dimensional vector residing in R^3, and it is just one of the infinitely many vectors in that space. If you have a vector of size one, say just the number 1.5, we put brackets around it to formally denote it as a vector of size one. Now, if we take a standard column vector and write it horizontally, it becomes a row vector. This operation is called a transpose. We denote the transpose of a column vector x as x^T. We can easily perform arithmetic on these vectors. You can add two vectors, like x and y, simply by adding them component-wise: x_1 + y_1, x_2 + y_2, and so on. However, this only works if they are of the exact same dimension; you obviously cannot add a 2D vector to a 3D vector. Similarly, you can multiply a vector by a scalar value, let's call it α. You just multiply every single component inside the vector by that scalar: αx_1, αx_2, αx_3.

    在人工智慧和機器學習中處理數據時，我們通常將資訊表示為向量。想像一下我們有一位客戶，我們收集了關於這個特定個人的1,000筆資訊。這些數據點可以整齊地組織成一個包含1,000個分量的向量。通常，當我們說「向量」時，我們指的是行向量。這個向量（我們稱之為 x）中的分量或元素，表示為 x_1 到 x_n。我們在數學上將 R^n 定義為 n 維歐幾里得空間，這只是一個標準符號，用來表示所有包含 n 個實數分量的可能向量的集合。例如，向量 [1, 2, 3] 是存在於 R^3 中的三維向量，它只是該空間中無限多個向量之一。如果你有一個大小為一的向量，比如數字 1.5，我們會在它兩邊加上括號，正式將其表示為大小為一的向量。現在，如果我們拿出一個標準的行向量並將其橫向書寫，它就會變成一個列向量。這個操作稱為轉置。我們將行向量 x 的轉置表示為 x^T。我們可以輕鬆地對這些向量進行算術運算。你可以將兩個向量（例如 x 和 y）相加，只需將它們的分量逐一相加即可：x_1 + y_1、x_2 + y_2，依此類推。然而，這只有在它們維度完全相同時才有效；你顯然不能將二維向量加到三維向量上。同樣，你可以將向量乘以一個純量值（我們稱之為 α）。你只需將向量內的每一個分量乘以該純量：αx_1、αx_2、αx_3。

  - [13:26:36 - 13:28:17] **The Dot Product and Geometric Similarity**

    Next is the dot product, also known as the scalar product. What exactly is a dot product between two vectors x and y in R^n? It is calculated by taking the transpose of the first vector and multiplying it by the second vector, written as x^T y. Since x^T is a 1 by n row vector and y is an n by 1 column vector, their product results in a 1 by 1 matrix, which is just a single scalar number. Mathematically, it is the summation of the component-wise products: Σ(x_i * y_i). Let me give you an example. Suppose we have a vector x as [-1, 2] and a vector y as [4, 2]. When you compute the dot product, you get (-1 * 4) + (2 * 2), which equals -4 + 4, resulting exactly in zero. Why is it significant when the dot product of two vectors is zero? Geometrically, a dot product of zero means that these two vectors are perfectly perpendicular to each other; they form a 90-degree angle. This geometric interpretation is incredibly useful. Eventually, when you represent customer data as vectors, you can calculate the angle between them. If the angle is zero degrees, it means the vectors are pointing in the exact same direction, indicating those two customers are highly similar. Conversely, if the vectors are perpendicular, the customers are entirely uncorrelated. Therefore, the smaller the angle between two vectors, the more similar those two data points are.

    接下來是內積，也稱為純量積。R^n 中兩個向量 x 和 y 之間的內積究竟是什麼？它的計算方法是取第一個向量的轉置並將其乘以第二個向量，寫為 x^T y。由於 x^T 是一個 1 乘 n 的列向量，而 y 是一個 n 乘 1 的行向量，它們的乘積會產生一個 1 乘 1 的矩陣，也就是一個單一的純量數字。在數學上，它是各分量乘積的總和：Σ(x_i * y_i)。讓我舉個例子。假設我們有一個向量 x 為 [-1, 2] 和一個向量 y 為 [4, 2]。當你計算內積時，你會得到 (-1 * 4) + (2 * 2)，等於 -4 + 4，結果剛好為零。當兩個向量的內積為零時，為什麼這很重要？在幾何學上，內積為零意味著這兩個向量彼此完全垂直；它們形成一個 90 度角。這種幾何解釋非常有用。最終，當你將客戶數據表示為向量時，你可以計算它們之間的角度。如果角度為零度，這意味著向量指向完全相同的方向，表明這兩個客戶高度相似。相反地，如果向量是垂直的，這些客戶就完全不相關。因此，兩個向量之間的角度越小，這兩個數據點就越相似。

  - [13:28:17 - 13:31:51] **Linear Independence and Its Application in Regression**

    I know some of you might have forgotten the exact meaning of linear independence and linear dependence from your previous linear algebra classes, but it is a critical concept. Here is the formal definition. Suppose we have m vectors, x_1 to x_m, in R^n. We say these vectors are linearly independent if the only way to satisfy the equation λ_1x_1 + λ_2x_2 + ... + λ_mx_m = 0 is if every single scalar coefficient (λ_1, λ_2, etc.) equals strictly zero. This summation is what we call a linear combination. If the only solution to making that combination equal to the zero vector is setting all coefficients to zero, then there is no redundant information among those vectors. Obviously, if some of those scalar coefficients are not zero and the equation still holds, then the vectors are linearly dependent. This means that at least one of the vectors can be perfectly expressed as a linear combination of the remaining vectors. If you throw away that dependent vector, you wouldn't lose any informational value because its information is already encoded in the others. Let's look at an example. If x_1 is [1, 0] and x_2 is [0, 1], they are clearly independent. The only way λ_1[1, 0] + λ_2[0, 1] equals [0, 0] is if both λ_1 and λ_2 are zero. But if we introduce x_3 as [3, 5], the set becomes dependent because x_3 is exactly equal to 3*x_1 + 5*x_2. Where do we encounter this in practice? When you are doing linear regression in machine learning. In regression, you try to explain a target variable y (like how much a customer will spend on a platform) using a combination of feature columns X (like demographic information, age, income). If the columns in your feature matrix X are linearly dependent, it means you have redundant features. Your model gets confused because it cannot uniquely assign weight to those features. So, checking for linear independence helps ensure each feature provides unique explanatory power.

    我知道你們中有些人可能已經忘記了之前線性代數課程中線性獨立和線性相關的確切含義，但這是一個關鍵概念。這是正式的定義。假設我們在 R^n 中有 m 個向量，x_1 到 x_m。如果滿足方程式 λ_1x_1 + λ_2x_2 + ... + λ_mx_m = 0 的唯一方法是每個純量係數（λ_1、λ_2 等）都嚴格等於零，我們就說這些向量是線性獨立的。這個總和就是我們所說的線性組合。如果讓該組合等於零向量的唯一解是將所有係數設為零，那麼這些向量之間就沒有冗餘資訊。顯然，如果其中一些純量係數不為零且方程式仍然成立，那麼這些向量就是線性相關的。這意味著至少有一個向量可以完美地表示為其餘向量的線性組合。如果你丟棄那個相關向量，你不會失去任何資訊價值，因為它的資訊已經編碼在其他向量中了。我們來看一個例子。如果 x_1 是 [1, 0] 且 x_2 是 [0, 1]，它們顯然是獨立的。讓 λ_1[1, 0] + λ_2[0, 1] 等於 [0, 0] 的唯一情況是 λ_1 和 λ_2 都是零。但如果我們引入 x_3 為 [3, 5]，這個集合就會變成相關的，因為 x_3 完全等於 3*x_1 + 5*x_2。我們在實務中會在哪裡遇到這個問題？當你在機器學習中進行線性迴歸時。在迴歸中，你試圖使用特徵列 X（如人口統計資訊、年齡、收入）的組合來解釋目標變數 y（例如客戶將在平台上花費多少錢）。如果你特徵矩陣 X 中的列是線性相關的，這意味著你有冗餘的特徵。你的模型會感到困惑，因為它無法為這些特徵唯一地分配權重。因此，檢查線性獨立性有助於確保每個特徵都提供獨特的解釋能力。

  - [13:31:51 - 13:35:49] **Matrices and Linear Systems of Equations**

    Matrices are also something you should know very well. A matrix is essentially an extension of vectors into a two-dimensional grid of numbers. A matrix A of size m by n has m rows and n columns. The elements inside are denoted as a_ij, where 'i' is the row index and 'j' is the column index. You will see equations of this specific form constantly: a system of linear equations. We want to find a vector of unknown variables, x, such that a_11*x_1 + a_12*x_2 + ... + a_1n*x_n equals some known value b_1. We have m of these equations because there are m rows, ending with a_m1*x_1 + ... = b_m. To write this compactly, we express it as Ax = b. Sometimes, you will also see it written equivalently as A^T x = b, depending on how the data is oriented. It just means you multiply the transpose of the matrix by the vector x. What is incredibly important to understand is the column interpretation of this system. We usually see matrix-vector multiplication as taking the dot product of rows with the vector, but you can also interpret Ax = b as finding a linear combination of the columns of matrix A. If we take the first column of A and multiply it by x_1, add it to the second column multiplied by x_2, all the way to the n-th column multiplied by x_n, the sum of all these scaled columns must equal the vector b. In the context of our previous discussion on regression, what is the meaning of this? Vector b is your dependent variable, what you are trying to predict—say, the spending levels of a survey participant. The columns of matrix A are your features, the distinct demographic variables. We are asking: can this y value be explained as a linear combination of the features we collected? If your features are useful, you will find a set of coefficients (the vector x) that perfectly, or at least closely, combinations those columns to match the target b.

    矩陣也是你們應該非常熟悉的東西。矩陣本質上是將向量擴展為數字的二維網格。一個大小為 m 乘 n 的矩陣 A 擁有 m 個列（rows）和 n 個行（columns）。裡面的元素表示為 a_ij，其中「i」是列索引，「j」是行索引。你們會經常看到這種特定形式的方程式：線性聯立方程式。我們希望找到一個未知變數向量 x，使得 a_11*x_1 + a_12*x_2 + ... + a_1n*x_n 等於某個已知值 b_1。我們有 m 個這樣的方程式，因為有 m 列，最後以 a_m1*x_1 + ... = b_m 結束。為了簡潔地寫出這個式子，我們將其表示為 Ax = b。有時，你也會看到它被等價地寫成 A^T x = b，這取決於數據的排列方式。這只意味著你將矩陣的轉置乘以向量 x。理解這個系統的「行（column）解釋」極為重要。我們通常將矩陣與向量的乘法看作是取列與向量的內積，但你也可以將 Ax = b 解釋為尋找矩陣 A 各行的線性組合。如果我們取 A 的第一行並將其乘以 x_1，加上乘以 x_2 的第二行，一直到乘以 x_n 的第 n 行，所有這些經過縮放的行的總和必須等於向量 b。在我們之前關於迴歸的討論背景下，這意味著什麼？向量 b 是你的應變數，也就是你試圖預測的東西——比如說，調查參與者的消費水準。矩陣 A 的行（columns）是你的特徵，即不同的人口統計變數。我們在問：這個 y 值能否被解釋為我們收集到的特徵的線性組合？如果你的特徵是有用的，你就會找到一組係數（向量 x），能完美地或至少非常接近地組合這些特徵行以匹配目標 b。

  - [13:35:49 - 13:40:34] **Matrix Multiplication, Transpose, and Symmetry**

    Let's take a look at a concrete example to solidify this. Suppose m equals 2, meaning we have two equations: 4x_1 - 2x_2 = -5, and 1x_1 + 3x_2 = 1. Our matrix A consists of the coefficients: the first column is [4, 1] and the second column is [-2, 3]. The right-hand side vector b is [-5, 1]. Geometrically, finding the solution to this 2D linear system means we are looking for a point (x_1, x_2) that lies perfectly on both lines simultaneously. The solution is literally the physical intersection of these two lines. If the two lines happen to be perfectly parallel, they will never intersect, meaning there is absolutely no solution to the system. Moving on, you can obviously add matrices A and B together, but only if they have the exact same dimensions. You simply add each corresponding component, a_ij + b_ij. Multiplication is slightly trickier. If matrix A is 2 by 3, and B is 3 by 2, they can be multiplied because the inner dimensions match (3 and 3). The resulting product matrix will take on the outer dimensions, becoming a 2 by 2 matrix. You compute this by taking the dot product of the rows of A with the columns of B. Remember, matrix multiplication is not a commutative operation. Matrix AB is fundamentally not equal to matrix BA. We also have the identity matrix, which acts like the number 1 for matrices; it has 1s strictly along the main diagonal and 0s everywhere else. The number of rows and columns must be the same for an identity matrix. We already mentioned the transpose operation earlier, where you exchange the rows and columns. It is vital to place the transpose correctly when working with algorithms. If A is a 2 by 4 matrix, its transpose A^T will be 4 by 2. Finally, an important concept is the symmetric matrix. A matrix is symmetric if it is a square matrix and equals its own transpose (A = A^T). This means the top half above the diagonal is a perfect mirror image of the bottom half. Recognizing that a matrix is symmetric is highly practical in computer science because you can save almost 50% on storage memory; you only need to store the diagonal and one half of the matrix, as the other half is identical.

Suppose we have a function f(x, y) = x^2 + 2y^3. It has two components. The partial derivative with respect to x is simply 2x, because the 2y^3 term is treated as a constant and disappears. The partial derivative with respect to y is 6y^2, as the x^2 term acts as a constant and drops out. We stack these together to form our gradient vector: [2x, 6y^2].

    現在，我們如何處理多變數函數？如果我們有一個從 R^n 映射到 R 的函數，我們定義偏導數。你相對於一個特定變數（比如 x_1）求導，同時保持所有其他變數（x_2、x_3 等）完全恆定。你只允許 x_1 改變一個很小的量 h，當 h 趨近於零時求極限。我們將所有這些偏導數收集到一個單一向量中，由一個倒三角形符號 (∇) 表示，我們稱之為梯度向量。梯度下降正是利用這個梯度向量來尋找沿著損失表面下降的最快路徑。有時，在處理向量值函數時，你也會聽到雅可比矩陣（Jacobian matrix）。我們來看一個你經常會遇到的關鍵形式：二次函數。在許多演算法中，如支援向量機（Support Vector Machines），普通的線性迴歸無法捕捉到複雜性，因此我們最佳化一個二次函數。任何一般的二次函數都可以用矩陣符號寫成 1/2 x^T A x + b^T x + c。請注意，在處理向量時，你絕對不能只寫「x^2」；你必須使用轉置把它寫出來，x^T x。這個多變數二次函數相對於向量 x 的梯度是什麼？如果你逐項進行詳細推導——你現在不需要嚴格要求自己去親自推導——你會發現梯度簡化後就只是 Ax + b。想想看，這與單變數微積分是如何對應的：1/2 ax^2 + bx 的導數就是 ax + b。它完美對應。我們來追蹤一個具體的例子。假設我們有一個函數 f(x, y) = x^2 + 2y^3。它有兩個分量。對 x 的偏導數簡單來說就是 2x，因為 2y^3 項被視為常數而消失。對 y 的偏導數是 6y^2，因為 x^2 項作為常數被消去了。我們將這些堆疊在一起，形成我們的梯度向量：[2x, 6y^2]。

  - [14:33:06 - 14:36:25] **The Sigmoid Function and Vanishing Gradients**

    Next is a very important function that I am sure you have seen. The sigmoid function is a crucial activation function in deep learning. Imagine you are trying to predict a target value. If the target value is continuous (like predicting salary or housing prices), you would use linear regression, predicting the output simply as a linear combination of your features X. But what if your problem is actually a classification problem? You want to predict whether an email is class 0 (not spam) or class 1 (spam). It is not a continuous regression problem. That is when we use logistic regression. We still calculate a linear combination of inputs, which we often call the "net" input (z or y). But instead of outputting that raw linear combination, we pass it through the sigmoid function, denoted mathematically as σ(y) = 1 / (1 + e^-y).

    ![](https://encrypted-tbn2.gstatic.com/licensed-image?q=tbn:ANd9GcQnqBBDwgVny27409A2UYMyVgweXDdBdigNNJ8G1WNw-EOFAXlEvc6eH0iYLv9j54U-xKc8MYwnpCJrL70)

    The input to this function can be anything on the x-axis, ranging from minus infinity to positive infinity. But observe the output bounds. When the input y becomes a very large positive number (approaching infinity), e^-y approaches zero, so the formula becomes 1 / (1 + 0), meaning the output smoothly approaches strictly 1. Conversely, when the input is a large negative number (minus infinity), e^-y becomes infinitely large, and 1 divided by a massive number approaches 0. Therefore, the sigmoid cleanly maps any real number strictly into a range between 0 and 1, outputting what we interpret as a probability. If your linear combination of inputs (the net signal) is strongly positive, the model confidently outputs close to 1 (class 1). If it's strongly negative, it outputs close to 0 (class 0). Now, I want you to pay attention to the derivative of this sigmoid function. It's a very simple and beautiful mathematical result that you can prove easily using the quotient rule. The derivative is exactly equal to the function's value multiplied by 1 minus the function's value: σ(y) * (1 - σ(y)). Because the sigmoid output is between 0 and 1, the maximum possible value of this derivative is only 0.25 (a quarter), which happens when the output is exactly 0.5. More importantly, when the net input signal is very strong—meaning the prediction is highly confident, either very close to 1 or very close to 0—look what happens to the derivative formula. It becomes 1 * (1 - 1) = 0, or 0 * (1 - 0) = 0. The derivative goes to zero! This creates a grave issue during neural network training known as the vanishing gradient problem. Since backpropagation multiplies these gradients together, if the derivative at a node hits 0, that zero multiplies backward, erasing the error signal. When the gradient goes to zero, the network completely stops updating its weights. Training halts entirely.

    接下來是一個非常重要的函數，我相信你們一定見過。Sigmoid 函數是深度學習中一個至關重要的激勵函數。想像一下你正在試圖預測一個目標值。如果目標值是連續的（比如預測薪資或房價），你會使用線性迴歸，簡單地將輸出預測為特徵 X 的線性組合。但如果你的問題實際上是一個分類問題呢？你想要預測一封電子郵件是類別 0（非垃圾郵件）還是類別 1（垃圾郵件）。這不是一個連續的迴歸問題。這時我們就要使用邏輯斯迴歸（logistic regression）。我們仍然計算輸入的線性組合，我們通常稱之為「淨」輸入（net input，z 或 y）。但我們不是直接輸出那個原始的線性組合，而是將它通過 sigmoid 函數，數學上表示為 σ(y) = 1 / (1 + e^-y)。這個函數的輸入可以是 x 軸上的任何值，範圍從負無限大到正無限大。但請觀察輸出的邊界。當輸入 y 變成一個非常大的正數（趨近於無限大）時，e^-y 趨近於零，所以公式變成 1 / (1 + 0)，這意味著輸出平滑地嚴格趨近於 1。相反地，當輸入是一個很大的負數（負無限大）時，e^-y 變得無限大，而 1 除以一個極大的數字會趨近於 0。因此，sigmoid 乾淨俐落地將任何實數嚴格映射到 0 和 1 之間的範圍內，輸出我們解讀為機率的值。如果你輸入的線性組合（淨訊號）是強烈的正值，模型就會自信地輸出接近 1（類別 1）。如果是強烈的負值，它就輸出接近 0（類別 0）。現在，我希望你們注意這個 sigmoid 函數的導數。這是一個非常簡單優美的數學結果，你可以使用除法法則輕鬆證明。導數完全等於該函數值乘以 1 減去該函數值：σ(y) * (1 - σ(y))。由於 sigmoid 輸出介於 0 和 1 之間，這個導數的最大可能值只有 0.25（四分之一），這發生在輸出剛好是 0.5 的時候。更重要的是，當淨輸入訊號非常強時——意味著預測高度自信，無論是非常接近 1 還是非常接近 0——看看導數公式會發生什麼事。它變成 1 * (1 - 1) = 0，或者 0 * (1 - 0) = 0。導數變成零了！這在神經網路訓練期間造成了一個嚴重的問題，被稱為梯度消失問題。因為反向傳播是將這些梯度乘在一起，如果某個節點的導數變成 0，那個零就會向後相乘，抹除掉誤差訊號。當梯度變成零時，網路就會完全停止更新其權重。訓練就會徹底停滯。

  - [14:36:25 - 14:40:28] **Applying Gradients in Practice and Wrap Up**

    Let's look at one final, interesting example to tie this together. Think of this as a very simple neural network consisting of just a single neuron utilizing a logistic sigmoid function. Suppose our net input y is a linear combination of weights and features: w_0 + 2w_1 - 3w_2. The features (our data point for a customer) are the fixed values [1, 2, -3]. We initialize our weights w_0, w_1, w_2 randomly, and our goal is to optimize them. We take the input, compute the linear combination, and apply the sigmoid function, giving us our model's prediction σ(y). Suppose this specific customer is actually labeled as class 1 in our dataset. We want our prediction to be 1. The error of our model is the difference between the true label (1) and our prediction σ(y). To optimize those weights via gradient descent, we must calculate the gradient of this error with respect to each weight. Doing so requires applying both the gradient concepts and the chain rule we just discussed. First, you take the derivative of the error metric with respect to the output, multiply it by the derivative of the sigmoid activation, and finally multiply that by the derivative of the linear combination with respect to the specific weight (which just leaves the feature value, like 2 or -3). This computation forms the gradient, and we update the weights by stepping in the negative direction of that gradient. Now, you might be asking yourself: why go through all these classical mathematical derivations? Especially today, when you can just use computer programs, Deep Learning libraries like PyTorch, or even ask ChatGPT to write the code for you. The reason is that having this classical view allows you to actually understand what is happening inside the black box. It's not just about letting the tools do it; it is about being able to verify if the output is correct and knowing how to troubleshoot when the model fails—like recognizing a vanishing gradient.     讓我們來看最後一個有趣的例子來總結一下。你可以把這想成一個非常簡單的神經網路，只包含一個使用邏輯斯 sigmoid 函數的單一神經元。假設我們的淨輸入 y 是權重和特徵的線性組合：w_0 + 2w_1 - 3w_2。特徵（我們客戶的數據點）是固定值 [1, 2, -3]。我們隨機初始化權重 w_0、w_1、w_2，而我們的目標是最佳化它們。我們接收輸入，計算線性組合，並應用 sigmoid 函數，得到我們模型的預測 σ(y)。假設這個特定客戶在我們的資料集中實際上被標記為類別 1。我們希望我們的預測是 1。我們模型的誤差就是真實標籤 (1) 與我們的預測 σ(y) 之間的差異。為了透過梯度下降最佳化這些權重，我們必須計算這個誤差相對於每個權重的梯度。這需要應用我們剛剛討論過的梯度概念和連鎖律。首先，你計算誤差指標相對於輸出的導數，將其乘以 sigmoid 激勵函數的導數，最後再將其乘以線性組合相對於特定權重的導數（這只會留下特徵值，例如 2 或 -3）。這個計算形成了梯度，然後我們透過沿著該梯度的負方向邁步來更新權重。現在，你可能會問自己：為什麼要經歷所有這些經典的數學推導？特別是在今天，你大可直接使用電腦程式、像 PyTorch 這樣的深度學習函式庫，或者甚至請 ChatGPT 幫你寫程式碼。原因在於，擁有這種經典的視角能讓你真正理解黑盒子內部發生了什麼事。這不僅僅是讓工具去執行；而是能夠驗證輸出是否正確，並在模型失敗時知道如何排除故障——例如識別出梯度消失。理解這些機制才是區分一般使用者和工程師的關鍵。好了，我今天就講到這裡。這些投影片中提供的數學基礎，對於我們在這個模組中將要處理的所有 AI 內容來說，已經綽綽有餘了。有任何問題嗎？沒有？好的，下週見。下週的課程長度大概差不多，肯定是三個小時，中間包含休息時間。祝各位這週順利！
