# Week 1
## Week 1 Summary
  - **Cultural Analytics vs. Cultural Informatics 文化分析学与文化信息学**
    - **Cultural Analytics:** The computational process of collecting, analyzing, and evaluating large collections of digitized cultural materials. It involves finding hidden patterns in large datasets (e.g., chronologically analyzing condominium names to track linguistic trends or scraping social media images).
    - **Cultural Informatics:** The **intentional** application of technology to cultural data to achieve a specific goal. Rather than just analyzing, it solves problems—like using technology to break down the "do not touch" barrier in museums to create engaging experiences for new audiences.
    - **文化分析学：** 收集、分析和评估大量数字化文化材料的计算过程。它涉及在大型数据集中寻找隐藏的模式（例如，按时间顺序分析公寓名称以追踪语言趋势或抓取社交媒体图像）。
    - **文化信息学：** **有意图地**将技术应用于文化数据以实现特定目标。它不仅仅是分析，而是解决问题——比如利用技术打破博物馆中“请勿触摸”的障碍，为新受众创造引人入胜的体验。
  - **Expanding Cultural Data Modalities 扩展文化数据模态**
    - **Sensory & Tactile Data:** Moving beyond purely visual mediums to include sonification (sound), smell, and tactile feedback. A prime example is adapting 2D paintings into tactile booklets with varied textures for the visually impaired.
    - **Intangible & Spatial Heritage:** Treating human movement as a dataset by using motion capture to preserve rare traditional dances. Additionally, digitizing historical maps allows for spatial data analysis, such as tracking urban heat evolution across decades.
    - **3D Environmental Scanning:** Utilizing comprehensive 3D scanning (both above and underwater) to digitally preserve endangered architectural structures like residential Kelongs.
    - **感官与触觉数据：** 超越纯粹的视觉媒介，包括听觉化（声音）、嗅觉和触觉反馈。一个典型的例子是将2D画作改编成具有不同纹理的触觉小册子，供视障人士使用。
    - **非物质与空间遗产：** 将人类运动视为一种数据集，通过使用动作捕捉来保存罕见的传统舞蹈。此外，将历史地图数字化可以进行空间数据分析，例如追踪几十年来城市高温的演变。
    - **3D 环境扫描：** 利用全面的3D扫描（包括水上和水下）对住宅奎笼等濒危建筑结构进行数字化保护。
  - **Core Technologies & Immersive Workflows 核心技术与沉浸式工作流程**
    - **Immersive Tech (AR/VR):** Utilizing augmented reality to layer interactive information over physical museum artifacts, and virtual reality to transport users directly into historical scenarios (like speaking with Confucius).
    - **AI Applications:** Leveraging generative AI for cultural restoration (like old photos) and **Emotion AI** to computationally map human feelings from text, micro-expressions, or voice, which is essential for building emotionally intelligent interfaces.
    - **沉浸式技术 (AR/VR)：** 利用增强现实将交互式信息叠加在实体博物馆文物上，并利用虚拟现实将用户直接带入历史场景（如与孔子对话）。
    - **AI 应用：** 利用生成式AI进行文化修复（如老照片），以及利用**情感AI**从文本、微表情或声音中计算映射人类情感，这对于构建具有情感智能的交互界面至关重要。
  - **Course Assessments & Studio Execution 课程评估与工作室执行**
    - **Reflective Logs:** Requires rigorous critical thinking that links class theory to personal insights. Surface-level summaries and AI-generated logs are strictly prohibited.
    - **Group Project Protocol:** A hands-on, studio-based workflow requiring teams to prototype either an "Immersive Storytelling" or "Reimagining Collections" experience. Success relies on transparent team dynamics and proactive networking with industry experts.
    - **反思日志：** 要求严谨的批判性思维，将课堂理论与个人见解联系起来。严禁表面化的总结和AI生成的日志。
    - **小组项目协议：** 一个以工作室为基础的实践工作流程，要求团队制作“沉浸式讲故事”或“重塑馆藏”体验的原型。成功依赖于透明的团队动力学和积极与行业专家建立联系。
## Week 1 Learning Tips
  Welcome to the start of a fascinating semester! As we dive into Cultural Informatics, I want you to focus heavily on the concept of **intentionality**. A common pitfall in this field is suffering from "shiny object syndrome"—applying AR, VR, or AI to a project simply because the technology is new and exciting. To truly master this material, always ask yourself the "why" before the "how." Are you using a 3D scanner just to make a cool digital copy, or are you doing it to democratize access to a fragile artifact that people are otherwise forbidden to touch? When you ground your technological choices in a clear cultural purpose, your projects will elevate from mere technical demonstrations to meaningful human experiences.

  欢迎来到这个精彩学期的开端！当我们深入学习文化信息学时，我希望你们能将重点放在意图（intentionality）这个概念上。这个领域常见的一个陷阱是患上“闪亮物体综合症”——仅仅因为技术新颖刺激，就把AR、VR或AI应用到项目中。为了真正掌握这些材料，在问“怎么做”之前，请始终问自己“为什么”。你使用3D扫描仪只是为了制作一个炫酷的数字副本，还是为了让人们能够接触到那些原本被禁止触摸的脆弱文物，从而实现访问的民主化？当你将技术选择建立在明确的文化目的之上时，你的项目就会从单纯的技术演示升华为有意义的人类体验。

  I also challenge you to completely rewire how you define "data." In typical tech environments, we are conditioned to view data strictly as numbers in a spreadsheet, lines of text, or visual labels. This week, we learned that the rhythm of a traditional dance, the smell of a local coffee shop, the texture of oil paint, and the structural integrity of a wooden Kelong are all incredibly rich datasets. As you begin conceptualizing your group projects and writing your reflective logs, look for the invisible data around you. If you can build an intuition for recognizing alternative data modalities—like sonification and tactile feedback—you will design far more inclusive and innovative prototypes.

  我还希望挑战你们彻底重构对“数据”的定义。在典型的技术环境中，我们习惯于将数据严格地视为电子表格中的数字、文本行或视觉标签。这周，我们了解到传统舞蹈的节奏、当地咖啡店的气味、油画的纹理以及木制奎笼的结构完整性，都是极其丰富的数据集。当你们开始构思小组项目和撰写反思日志时，请寻找周围那些无形的数据。如果你能培养出识别替代数据模态（如听觉化和触觉反馈）的直觉，你就能设计出更具包容性和创新性的原型。

  Finally, let’s talk about your approach to the reflective learning logs and the governance of AI. Do not treat ethics, bias, and representation as a simple compliance checklist that you mark off at the end of a project. Instead, view them as core design constraints that shape your product from day one. When reflecting on the material, don't just summarize what I said in the lecture. I want to see you wrestle with the concepts. How does mapping emotion through AI potentially misrepresent certain cultural expressions? What happens when a generative AI model is trained on biased historical data? By confronting these uncomfortable gray areas actively in your logs, you'll develop the rigorous, master's-level critical thinking required to succeed in this course and in the industry.

  最后，我们来谈谈你们对待反思性学习日志和AI治理的态度。不要把伦理、偏见和代表性看作是在项目结束时勾选的简单合规检查表。相反，应将它们视为从第一天起就塑造你产品的核心设计约束。在反思学习材料时，不要仅仅总结我在讲座中说过的话。我希望看到你们与这些概念进行思想上的碰撞。通过AI映射情感，是否有可能曲解某些文化表达？当生成式AI模型在有偏见的历史数据上进行训练时，会发生什么？通过在日志中积极面对这些令人不适的灰色地带，你们将培养出严谨的、硕士级别的批判性思维，这是在本课程和整个行业中取得成功所必需的。

  - [19:07:20 - 19:15:20] **Defining Cultural Analytics and Informatics**

    After confirming the class didn't need a break just yet, the lecturer transitioned into a deeper theoretical dive into cultural analytics and informatics. He explained that while he used to teach both concepts together, he eventually realized analytics is dense enough to require its own separate course. Cultural analytics involves applying computational methods to large, digitized collections of cultural materials—like art, literature, and social media images—to identify underlying patterns. While applying these techniques to cultural data is a relatively new field that has only truly emerged in the last five years, the underlying technology itself is not new. To illustrate, he pointed to the massive CCTV surveillance network in London. Because London is a high-profile city frequently targeted by attacks, it possesses one of the highest numbers of CCTV cameras in the world. The technology used to capture data on people's habits and recognize behavioral patterns in London has existed for years; what is novel is turning that computational power toward cultural texts and artifacts. Informatics, on the other hand, is the intentional application of technology to that data to achieve a specific goal. The key word is *intentional*—technology shouldn't be used just because it looks fun. For example, a core intention could be creating new audiences. The lecturer shared a real problem he faces in the NUS library: they have massive collections of printed books that are not available digitally. He concluded by noting that because these fields are so new, there are no traditional textbooks available, though they do reference Lev Manovich's seminal book on Cultural Analytics as a comprehensive guide.

    The lecturer then presented a fascinating cultural informatics project spearheaded by the National Heritage Board dealing with endangered cultural heritage: the documentation of Kelongs. For context, Kelongs are traditional wooden houses built on stilts directly above the water. Living on a Kelong requires immense physical labor and specialized survival skills; residents must constantly reinforce the structure, and if a stray canoe crashes into the stilts during a storm, they must physically dive into the sea to repair the damage. What makes this project groundbreaking is that they did not just scan the house above the water; they conducted extensive underwater documentation to capture the submerged stilt structures as well. The lecturer expressed his awe, noting that in all his years working in cultural documentation, this was the first time he had seen such thorough underwater technological capture applied to a heritage site.

## Week 2 Summary
  - **Quantification of Culture and the Culture Cycle 文化的量化与文化周期**
    - **English Keyword 1:** **Quantification as Summarization:** The act of reducing highly complex cultural expressions into accessible, measurable metrics (e.g., box office sales). While it aids in resource allocation and benchmarking, it carries a heavy risk of oversimplification and often fails to capture true artistic or well-being impact.
    - **English Keyword 2:** **The Culture Cycle:** A continuous, cyclical process consisting of Creation (research and ideation), Production (bringing concepts to life), Dissemination (where data is heavily utilized for marketing and hosting decisions), and Consumption/Participation (where audience KPIs directly inform future creations).
    - **中文关键词 1：** **量化即总结：** 将高度复杂的文化表达简化为易于理解和衡量的指标（如票房销量）的行为。虽然它有助于资源分配和基准测试，但带有严重的过度简化风险，并且通常无法捕捉到真正的艺术或福祉影响。
    - **中文关键词 2：** **文化周期：** 一个连续循环的过程，包括创作（研究与构思）、制作（将概念变为现实）、传播（在此阶段大量使用数据进行营销和托管决策）以及消费/参与（观众的KPI直接指导未来的创作）。
  - **Cultural Frameworks and Dimensions 文化框架与维度**
    - **English Keyword 1:** **UNESCO Framework & Cultural Onion:** The framework maps cultural landscapes by differentiating tangible (buildings) and intangible (music) heritage. The "Onion" model visually layers culture from explicit, visible artifacts (food, clothes) down to articulated values, and finally to implicit, structural basic assumptions (e.g., respect for hierarchy).
    - **English Keyword 2:** **Hofstede's Cultural Dimensions:** A macro-level framework conceptualizing global differences through indices like Power Distance, Individualism vs. Collectivism, Masculinity vs. Femininity, Uncertainty Avoidance, and Long/Short-Term Orientation.
    - **English Keyword 3:** **World Values Survey (WVS):** A longitudinal methodology tracking cultural shifts over decades. It maps countries on an evolving grid with axes for Traditional vs. Secular-Rational values, and Survival vs. Self-Expression values.
    - **English Keyword 4:** **Framework Pros & Cons:** Hofstede is excellent for broad, macro-global comparisons but fails at the micro-level. It suffers from overgeneralizing whole nations, relies on static 1970s corporate data, and is vulnerable to social desirability bias (where subjects answer based on perceived expectations rather than reality). WVS solves the static issue by tracking generational evolution.
    - **中文关键词 1：** **联合国教科文组织框架与文化洋葱：** 该框架通过区分有形（建筑）和无形（音乐）遗产来描绘文化图景。“洋葱”模型直观地将文化分层，从明确可见的人工制品（食物、衣物）深入到表达出的价值观，最后是隐性的、结构性的基本假设（如对等级的尊重）。
    - **中文关键词 2：** **霍夫斯泰德文化维度：** 一个宏观层面的框架，通过权力距离、个人主义与集体主义、男性化与女性化、不确定性规避以及长期/短期导向等指数来概念化全球差异。
    - **中文关键词 3：** **世界价值观调查 (WVS)：** 一种纵向的方法论，追踪数十年来的文化变迁。它在一个不断演变的网格上绘制国家位置，坐标轴为传统与世俗理性价值观，以及生存与自我表达价值观。
    - **中文关键词 4：** **框架优缺点：** 霍夫斯泰德模型在广泛的宏观全球比较中表现出色，但在微观层面却失效。它的缺陷在于过度泛化整个国家，依赖于20世纪70年代静态的企业数据，并且容易受到社会期望偏差的影响（受访者基于预期的期望而非现实来回答）。WVS通过追踪代际演变解决了静态数据的问题。
  - **Immersive Media Project Workflows 沉浸式媒体项目工作流**
    - **English Keyword 1:** **Accessible Prototyping Platforms:** Tools like ThingLink (utilizing 360-degree captures with interactive informational nodes) and Artsteps (free-roaming 3D virtual galleries) provide efficient workflows for spatial storytelling without requiring heavy backend coding.
    - **English Keyword 2:** **Advanced Interventions:** High-level cultural tech integrations involve WebAR (e.g., 8th Wall for anchoring digital elements in physical spaces), Projection Mapping (respecting structural constraints of historical architecture), and multi-sensory haptics/olfactory systems.
    - **English Keyword 3:** **AI Curation:** Generative AI should only be used to generate first drafts of visuals or text. It MUST be heavily edited and cross-checked by human historical custodians to ensure the integrity of the cultural data.
    - **中文关键词 1：** **易于上手的原型设计平台：** 像ThingLink（利用带有交互式信息节点的360度全景捕捉）和Artsteps（可自由漫游的3D虚拟画廊）这样的工具，为空间叙事提供了高效的工作流，而无需繁重的后端编码。

## Week 2 Learning Tips
  Welcome to the fascinating intersection of culture and data! As you review this week's material, I want you to build a strong intuition for *why* we quantify culture. A common conceptual pitfall is treating quantification simply as a rigid math exercise or a basic compliance checklist. Instead, remember that metrics like box office sales or the Hofstede indices are proxies. They are highly effective for macro-level benchmarking, but they fall apart if you blindly apply them to micro-level, individual interactions or local subcultures. Always ask yourself: "What is the specific purpose of this tool?" before using a global framework to analyze a nuanced local phenomenon.

  欢迎来到文化与数据这个迷人的交汇点！在复习本周的材料时，我希望你们能对我们“为什么”要量化文化建立起强烈的直觉。一个常见的概念误区是，仅仅把量化看作是一项死板的数学练习或基本的合规检查表。相反，请记住，像票房收入或霍夫斯泰德指数这样的指标只是替代变量。它们在宏观层面的基准测试中非常有效，但如果你盲目地将它们应用于微观层面的个体互动或本地亚文化，就会彻底失效。在使用全球框架来分析微妙的本地现象之前，永远要问自己：“这个工具的具体目的是什么？”

  When studying theoretical frameworks like the World Values Survey or Hofstede's dimensions, do not just memorize the axes. Focus on the fundamental differences in their data methodologies. Hofstede provides a static snapshot based on older corporate surveys, making it susceptible to social desirability bias. WVS, on the other hand, is longitudinal and captures dynamic generational shifts. To truly master these theories, actively look for modern examples—like the "ugly is beautiful" trend in modern animation mentioned in class—and analyze how they challenge outdated, static models. Culture is a living organism, not a fixed data point.

  在学习像世界价值观调查或霍夫斯泰德维度这样的理论框架时，不要只死记硬背那些坐标轴。要把重点放在它们数据方法论的根本区别上。霍夫斯泰德提供的是基于早期企业调查的静态快照，这使其容易受到社会期望偏差的影响。另一方面，WVS是纵向的，捕捉了动态的代际变迁。为了真正掌握这些理论，请主动寻找现代的例子——比如课堂上提到的现代动画中“审丑”的趋势——并分析它们是如何挑战过时的静态模型的。文化是一个有生命的有机体，而不是一个固定的数据点。

  Finally, regarding your immersive media projects, focus on the storytelling rather than getting bogged down by overly complex software. It is easy to fall into the trap of wanting to build a massive game engine environment or a real-time location-based AR app from scratch. Unless you already have significant coding experience, I strongly encourage you to leverage accessible platforms like ThingLink or Artsteps. Your immediate goal is to prototype a proof-of-concept that demonstrates how your cultural narrative works in space. Respect the constraints of the technology, avoid over-scoping, and let your thorough historical research drive the innovation.

  最后，关于你们的沉浸式媒体项目，请把重点放在叙事上，而不是陷入过度复杂的软件中。大家很容易陷入一种陷阱，想要从零开始构建一个庞大的游戏引擎环境或基于实时位置的AR应用程序。除非你已经有了丰富的编程经验，否则我强烈建议你利用像ThingLink或Artsteps这样易于上手的平台。你当前的目标是制作一个概念验证的原型，以此展示你的文化叙事是如何在空间中运作的。尊重技术的局限性，避免项目范围过大，让你详尽的历史研究来驱动创新。
